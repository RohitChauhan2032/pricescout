MOBILE BUYING GUIDE

How to choose a smartphone
in 2026

Skip the marketing noise. These decisions will actually
affect your everyday experience.

Updated 18 September 2026 · 8 min read

1. Start with how you use your phone

The best phone is not the one with the longest specification sheet. Think about the tasks you repeat every day.
Compare real usage, not only specifications.

QUICK RECOMMENDATION 8GB RAM and a good OLED screen suit most buyers.

2. Choose the right processor tier

Mid-range processors are now fast enough for everyday work. Pay extra only for demanding games or video editing.
Read test results and compare real-world value.

3. Don’t judge cameras by megapixels

Sensor quality, image processing and stabilisation matter more than the headline number.
Read test results and compare real-world value.
